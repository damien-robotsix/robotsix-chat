cognee is now pinned to an exact version (`cognee==1.4.0`) and the kuzu-era graph self-heal has been removed. cognee swaps its embedded graph engine between releases — 1.4 replaced kuzu with its own ladybug engine — and this codebase manipulates those on-disk stores directly, so an unnoticed engine change silently destroyed the live knowledge graph on every startup. With the engine pinned, the shadow/WAL heal, its healable-error retry wrapper and the kuzu error patterns are all dead code for a store that no longer exists, and deleting a ladybug `.wal` actively discards its crash-recovery journal. Roughly 230 lines of graph-destroying recovery logic are gone; the guards that keep the SQLite relational store, the LanceDB vector store and now the ladybug graph untouched at startup remain as regression tests.
