The autonomous assistant now honors monitor-pause recommendations when a tracked ticket is blocked on human review (`human_mr_approval` / `human_issue_approval`): it pauses the monitor instead of leaving it polling, since a human-review wait makes no progress through further polling and the system notifies via merge detection and ticket-update events (a paused monitor resumes automatically on a tracked-ticket update). It also no longer re-announces an unchanged human-review wait status — it reports the ticket, gate, and required human action once, then stays silent until the ticket actually changes state or the operator asks.
