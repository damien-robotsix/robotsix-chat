Autonomous monitors no longer treat queued-ticket inactivity on a serial board as an immediate stall. A new `autonomous.queue_tolerance_runs_before_escalation` setting (default 3) tells a periodic monitor how many consecutive zero-activity cycles to accept as queue wait — while earlier-queued tickets are actively being processed — before escalating a possible stall. This prevents the false stall alarms that were auto-stopping monitors and forcing manual respawns when the board was simply working through the wave one ticket at a time.
