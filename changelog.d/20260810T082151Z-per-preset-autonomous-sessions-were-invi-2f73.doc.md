Documented the `autonomous` bootstrap-owner prefix expansion in `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/user-guide/autonomous-sessions.md`: the session-list handler treats `owner_id=autonomous` as a query over the whole autonomous namespace, merging sessions from `autonomous` and every `autonomous:*` sub-scope, and only the bootstrap owner is eligible for lazy default creation — per-preset sub-scopes are owned by the runner and never created on read.
